    Pause Point · Wearable Speech Simulation

    A prototype of an assistive facial wearable that listens to everyday speech and shows a few simple keywords and picture cues, so a child can follow what is being said.

    Person speaks→Wearable hears→Speech to text→Key words picked out→Keywords + icons shown
    1. Walk around the school: classroom, corridor and playground.
    2. Go up to a person and press E to hear them. The wearable view shows the keywords.
    3. Turn on the microphone to try live speech (where the browser supports it).

    Prototype demonstration only — not a clinical, diagnostic or therapeutic tool. The microphone stays off until you switch it on, and this page does not record or store audio.

    On Meta Quest, press Enter VR (bottom right).
